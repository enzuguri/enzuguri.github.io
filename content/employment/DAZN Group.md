Led a 50+ person engineering organisation, managing leaders, principals, and developers. Accountable for technical direction across TV application development for Android, iOS, web, and embedded platforms. Developed company-wide initiatives for employee experience, progression, health, communication, and scalability.
Key activities included:
- Restructured the organisation from 7 to 10 teams using Team Topologies techniques, aligning teams to ownership boundaries and introducing full-stack working to reduce dependencies which improved the rate at which we onboarded new TV platforms.
- Budget management including headcount, hiring, and promotions to manage the running cost and rewards structure of my team. Redistributed specialist capabilities across teams during reorganisations and leadership transitions.
- Outsourcing teams and individual contractor management to leverage project expansions such as DAZN native runtimes (WASM) and new TV device variants.
- Evaluated build-vs-buy for internal infrastructure projects to focus on core business propositions.
- Stakeholder and inter-department management across all leadership functions (Product, HR, program management) to resolve disputes across technical choices, business timelines, and ownership and set expectations for unified working.
- Process improvement through regular cross-team roadmap reviews using a shared tracking process to improve visibility and co-ordination between teams, overall team health
- Accountable technical lead on DAZN fail-over micro-experience "emergency mode": a lightweight replica of core DAZN functionality to be used in extreme outages that would survive high traffic.
- Hands on working experience to gain first-hand experience of developer troubles in Haxe, node.js/Serverless, Typescript, Roku Brightscript, Kotlin, and Swift
